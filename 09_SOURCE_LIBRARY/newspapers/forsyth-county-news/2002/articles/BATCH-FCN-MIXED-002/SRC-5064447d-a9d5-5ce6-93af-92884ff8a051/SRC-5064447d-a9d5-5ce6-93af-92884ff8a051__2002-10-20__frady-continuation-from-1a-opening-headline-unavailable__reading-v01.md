### Full composite-sketch caption

Investigators are seeking the public’s help in identifying two possible witnesses in the 1997 murder of Levi Frady. The first person was seen walking along Little Mill Road at 6:30 p.m. on the day the boy was first missed. The man is described as white in his late 50s, with a scruffy, gray beard. He was wearing a faded blue baseball cap. He walked in a stooped fashion. The second potential witness, right, was seen in the Dawson Forest Wildlife Management Area. He is described as white, 45-55, clean shaven, 6-feet tall, slim build with white or gray hair. He was driving a 1980 to 1985 medium blue Toyota, pick-up truck with a white camper shell over the bed of the vehicle. Anyone with information is asked to call (800) 432-9284 or by write: Levi Frady Case 189, Hwy.53 West, Suite 201, Dawsonville, GA 30534.
