                The Forsyth County community iscontinuing on its                                                                    
      growthpattern and, because ofthat growth, some individuals maintain the                                                       
      county needs to move to the next level in addressing the “growingpains”                                                       
      being experienced by Cumming and Forsyth County.                                                                              
        One area formoving ahead is water. The contract talks between the                                                           
      County Commission and Cumming officials related tothe county obtain-                                                          
      ingwater from the city to expand the availability to water supplies across                                                    
      the county is one example.                                                                                                    
        Another suggested area forforward motion is wastewater treatment.                                                           
      With growth,residential development and commercial and industrial                                                             
      development, the need for sewerage enhancements isbeing stressed.                                                             
      Septic tanks are not suitable forsome development and proliferation of                                                        
      septic tanks in small-lot developments generate concerns. This is one area                                                    
      addressed in the Master Land Use Plan draft which is the subject of                                                           
      upcoming community meetings which begin on Tuesday. (See related                                                              
      story on Page 1A).                                                                                                            
        Yet another segment ofgovernment offered forpossible change tobet-                                                          
      ter serve the growing population both residential and business is law                                                         
                         -                 -                                                                                        
      enforcement. Creation of acounty-wide police department is being                                                              
      offered by the Forsyth County Board ofCommissioners as a July 21 ref-                                                         
      erendum issue.                                                                                                                
        Because there has been little discussion about the issue ofacounty-                                                         
      widepolice force todate, the Forsyth County News has been presenting                                                          
      different perspectives of law enforcement organization related by individ-                                                    
      uals who have been part of some ofthe changes which have occurred in                                                          
      other communities.                                                                                                            
        The information is being offered to provide alaunching pad for dis-                                                         
      cussion and to open communication in the community about the issue                                                            
      which willbe athand. Dialogue on such an issue any issue which has -                                                          
      the potential of making significant change which citizens need to under-                                                      
      stand is vital. -                                                                                                             
        That is why we are offering information about the workings oflaw                                                            
      enforcement operations in surrounding counties and other communities                                                          
      which have experienced similar orstrikingly different decisions. Itis                                                         
      important to lookbeyond your geographic borders in search ofknowl-                                                            
      edge ifyou are to avoid reinventing a wheel which fails to meet your                                                          
      transportation needs.                                                                                                         
        Ifwe look, leant and discuss issues, the answers willhopefully be
