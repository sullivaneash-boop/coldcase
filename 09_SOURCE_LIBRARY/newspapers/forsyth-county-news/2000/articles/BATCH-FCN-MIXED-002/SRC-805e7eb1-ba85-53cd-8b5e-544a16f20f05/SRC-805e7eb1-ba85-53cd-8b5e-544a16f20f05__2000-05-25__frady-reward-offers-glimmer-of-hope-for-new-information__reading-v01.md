## Full extant source text

<!-- P001 -->
The death of Levi Frady remains an injustice that haunts the Forsyth County community. Thanks to the generosity of two local businessmen, there is now a better chance of solving one of the county’s most horrific mysteries.

<!-- P002 -->
Last week, Tommy Bagwell, owner of American Proteins, and automobile dealer Bob Davis each agreed to add $50,000 to the reward being offered for information about the Levi Frady murder.

<!-- P003 -->
We can only hope that the addition of $100,000 to the existing reward fund, bringing the total to approximately $125,000, will provide the incentive necessary to unveil new clues in the murder.

<!-- P004 -->
A North Forsyth Middle School student, 11-year-old Levi disappeared while riding his bicycle Oct. 22, 1997. Deer hunters found his body the next day. The youngster had been shot to death.

<!-- P005 -->
Investigators from Dawson and Forsyth counties, as well as the GBI, have worked diligently to find the killer.

<!-- P006 -->
Forsyth County Sheriff Denny Hendrix recently formed a new task force to investigate Levi’s death, reviving interest in the case and resulting in the posting of the increased reward.

<!-- P007 -->
The newly promised reward money will be added to that already pledged by many others, including the governor’s office, Forsyth County Commission, Forsyth Sheriff’s Department, Dawson County officials and community leaders, and several private individuals.

<!-- P008 -->
The $100,000 added to the reward fund last week serves as a strong reminder that the case remains near to the hearts of many in Forsyth County and, at the same time, demonstrates the generosity and community spirit of the two individuals who have promised to make the funds available.

<!-- P009 -->
For that they are to be commended.

<!-- P010 -->
At the press conference announcing the new reward money, Bagwell noted that he has had a lengthy relationship with Frady family members.

<!-- P011 -->
“I have a long history with the family. I hope this helps,” Bagwell said.

<!-- P012 -->
Davis said he hoped his donation “helps bring justice to Levi Frady.”

<!-- P013 -->
So do we all.
