<!-- P001 -->
Oct. 22, 1997

<!-- P002 -->
• 3:30 p.m. - 4:12 p.m. Levi Frady, 11, departs from North Forsyth Middle School and arrives at his Burruss Mill Road home after getting off the school bus. Note: Levi’s twin sister Laci remains at school awaiting her mother, Marilyn Parkman, to pick her up at approximately 5:30 p.m.

<!-- P003 -->
• 4:12 p.m. Levi calls his mother at her workplace in Alpharetta, and tells her that he is going over to visit a friend on Noah Road and is also going by another friend’s residence. His mother tells Levi to be home by 6:30 p.m.

<!-- P004 -->
• 4:12 p.m. - 5 p.m. A couple walking on Little Mill Road sees Levi riding his bicycle past their driveway heading in the direction of another couple’s residence (on Little Mill Road). They observe that Levi has his book bag strapped on his back.

<!-- P005 -->
• 4:15 p.m. - 4:30 p.m. Another neighborhood resident sees Levi riding his bicycle traveling down Picklesimer Road.

<!-- P006 -->
• 4:30 - 5 p.m. Another neighborhood resident sees Levi riding his bike in the direction of Levi’s friend’s house.

<!-- P007 -->
• 4:40 p.m. - 5:45 p.m. Levi and his friend (friend #1) ride their bicycles over to another friend’s (friend #2) residence and play on the trampoline. During the visit, friend #2 invites Levi to go out to eat with his family. Friend #1 does not have the money to go and declines. Levi does have money with him and calls his residence to talk with his mother. She is not at home.

<!-- P008 -->
• 4:55 p.m. His mother leaves work en route to school to pick up her daughter.

<!-- P009 -->
• 5:30 p.m. - 5:45 p.m. Levi’s mother and sister arrive home bringing carry-out food from a local fast food restaurant.

<!-- P010 -->
• 5:45 p.m. - 6 p.m. Friend #2 and his family leave their residence en route to the restaurant to eat. Levi and friend #1 leave the residence at the same time en route back to the residence of friend #1. Note: Levi leaves his book bag on the patio of the residence of friend #2.

<!-- P011 -->
• 6:25 p.m. - 6:35 p.m. A witness is traveling down Little Mill Road in his Ford Bronco pulling a trailer. As he is approaching Frog’s Creek, he meets a beige F-150 truck followed by an Oldsmobile or Pontiac station wagon with ladders and a ladder rack. He stops to let the truck and station wagon go by. As he continues traveling, he meets two boys coming down a hill on their bicycles on the right side of the road. He then continues up the hill and sees a man walking down the hill on the left side of the road. The man has been picking up cans. (See composite sketch A)

<!-- P012 -->
• 6 p.m. - 6:38 p.m. Levi remains with friend #1, and at 6:38 p.m., they travel over to another residence to use the telephone. Levi’s friend remains outside the residence, and Levi calls home and talks with his sister. Note: Levi’s mother is in the bathtub and tells her daughter to tell Levi to come straight home. Levi says that he does not want to ride his bicycle home because it is getting dark and Levi mentions being afraid of some of the dogs he would encounter on his way home.

<!-- P013 -->
• 6:45 p.m. - 6:50 p.m. Levi leaves the residence of friend #1 on his bicycle traveling in the direction of Little Mill Road. Levi travels in this direction to avoid encountering the dogs on the other route, which would have been Picklesimer Road (the direction he initially came when traveling to his friend’s house after 4:12 p.m.)

<!-- P014 -->
• 6:50 p.m. A resident sees Levi traveling on his bike down Little Mill Road heading in the direction of the residence of friend #2. Note: the resident observed a small black dog following Levi.

<!-- P015 -->
• 6:52 p.m. - 7 p.m. A motorist, a stranger in the community who was lost, drives past a man described as being between 35 and 60 years old, having grayish black or light brown hair (scraggly and unkept). She observes that the man is wearing possibly blue jeans and a white or light colored shirt with the sleeves rolled up. She stops and attempts to ask the man for directions. The man does not respond to her when she says, “Mister.” The man walks off into the woods immediately after she speaks. The location where the man walks off into the woods is located at a resident’s driveway, which is within a few hundred feet of where Levi’s bike was ultimately found. She did not see a bicycle lying on the side of the road in that area during that time.

<!-- P016 -->
• 7 p.m. Levi’s mother calls Levi’s friend’s residence looking for her son but gets no answer.

<!-- P017 -->
• 7 p.m. - 7:15 p.m. Levi’s mother and sister leave their residence en route to look for Levi. Note: The mother’s boyfriend, Tim Tatum, pulls into the driveway as they are leaving. She tells her boyfriend that she is going to look for Levi because he has not come home yet. During this search, Levi’s sister sees a bike that looks like the one belonging to her brother lying on the side of the road at a driveway on Little Mill Road about a half mile from Levi’s home. She tells her mother, but her mother does not hear her. The mother travels to Levi’s friend’s house and after checking there, returns home. Levi’s sister does not mention seeing the bike again.

<!-- P018 -->
• 7:34 p.m. Levi’s mother and sister are back at their residence, and Levi’s sister calls her friend and tells her that Levi has not come home.

<!-- P019 -->
8:01 p.m. The family of Levi’s friend #2 purchases items at the Kmart in Cumming after the family went out to eat and writes a check.

<!-- P020 -->
Oct. 23, 1997

<!-- P021 -->
• 2 a.m. Levi’s mother wakes up to check on Levi. Levi is not home and she again leaves her residence in her vehicle and drives around on the road looking for Levi. She does not see the bicycle at a resident’s driveway.

<!-- P022 -->
• 5:15 a.m. A resident leaves for work and sees Levi’s bicycle at the end of his driveway on the edge adjacent to Little Mill Road.

<!-- P023 -->
• 6 a.m. A resident leaves for work and sees Levi’s bicycle in the same location as her husband did. Note: Neither of the residents moved the bicycle.

<!-- P024 -->
• 6:45 a.m. The residents’ children leave their residence and walk down to the end of the driveway to wait for the school bus. They see the bicycle at the end of the driveway adjacent to Little Mill Road and place it in the ditch alongside the driveway (where it is ultimately found).

<!-- P025 -->
• 7:02 a.m. Levi’s sister talks to her father by phone but does not tell him that Levi is missing.

<!-- P026 -->
• 7:30 a.m. Levi’s mother telephones the parents of Levi’s friend and tells them she is looking for Levi.

<!-- P027 -->
• 7:50 a.m. Levi’s mother returns to Levi’s friend’s residence and again looks for Levi. Note: During this time, Levi’s grandfather and other relatives are out looking for Levi. They find the bicycle in the ditch located adjacent to a driveway and pick the bike up and take it over to Levi’s mother’s house. The family discusses the matter and decides to take the bicycle back to its location before law enforcement arrives.

<!-- P028 -->
• 8 a.m. Levi’s mother files a missing person report with the Forsyth County Sheriff’s Office on her son Levi.

<!-- P029 -->
• 9:30 a.m. Levi’s mother calls her boyfriend at his place of employment and tells him that Levi is missing. He leaves work and comes to the search area.

<!-- P030 -->
• 10:15 a.m. Levi’s mother contacts Levi’s father, Milton Frady, and apprises him of the situation.

<!-- P031 -->
• 10:30 a.m. Ground search in the Little Mill area begins.

<!-- P032 -->
• 2 p.m. The body of Levi Frady is found in Dawson Forest Game Management Area by hunters. The area is near an abandoned Lockheed plant. Levi had been shot three times.

<!-- P033 -->
• 10:48 p.m. A hunter from Dawson Forest is interviewed, and he gives a description of a small blue truck with a white camper being seen in an area close to the crime scene at approximately 10 a.m. this same date. He also gives a description of a man dressed in a dress shirt and attire, which seemed out of place in Dawson Forest. (See composite sketch B)
