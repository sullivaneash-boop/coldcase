He was only eleven
So full of life and fun
Why do children have to pay
For things they’ve never done

He was only eleven
And had his whole future ahead
Birthday parties and school dances
But now he is dead

<!-- P003 -->
He was only eleven
He didn’t seem to have a care
But now when I go to get him
I realize he’s not there

<!-- P004 -->
He was only eleven
Are children easy prey?
They are so trusting
They believe what you say

<!-- P005 -->
He was only eleven
What did he have to give?
Not anything you wanted
Why didn’t you let him live?

<!-- P006 -->
He was only eleven
And everyone has to die
We’re happy he’s in Heaven
But missing him makes us cry
