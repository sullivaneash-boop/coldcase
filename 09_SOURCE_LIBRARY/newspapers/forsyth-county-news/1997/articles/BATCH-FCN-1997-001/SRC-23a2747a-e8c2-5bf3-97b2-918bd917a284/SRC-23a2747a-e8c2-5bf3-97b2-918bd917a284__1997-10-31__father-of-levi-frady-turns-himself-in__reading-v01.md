Authorities investigating the murder of 11-year-old Levi Frady may have gotten a break in their investigation.

<!-- P002 -->
Although investigators say they still aren’t ready to make an arrest in the case, they finally got a chance to interview the boy’s father, Milton Frady, after he turned himself in to Forsyth authorities on non-related charges Wednesday morning.

<!-- P003 -->
Levi was shot to death sometime between 6 p.m. Wednesday, Oct. 22 and 2:30 p.m. the following day. His body was found by a group of Gilmer County deer hunters on state wildlife management land in a wooded Dawson County forest.

<!-- P004 -->
While remaining quiet about specifics of the interview, Dawson County Sheriff Billy Carlisle said agents were able to get some helpful information from the long-awaited interview, saying Frady has “cleared up some areas where we had questions.” Agents began following up on those leads Wednesday afternoon.

<!-- P005 -->
But as of Wednesday night, Carlisle said an arrest in the case was not imminent, unless other information surfaced. He says no one has been eliminated as a suspect.

<!-- P006 -->
Atlanta attorney Michael Hauptman was by his client’s side when Frady was interviewed at the Forsyth County Detention Center by agents from the Georgia Bureau of Investigation and the Forsyth and Dawson counties sheriffs’ departments.
