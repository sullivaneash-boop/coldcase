## Full extant article text

<!-- P001 -->
Investigators searching for 11-year-old Levi Frady’s assailant say they have not settled on a suspect in the case, but Dawson County Sheriff Billy Carlisle says agents won’t stop until the person responsible is behind bars.

<!-- P002 -->
“We’re still out there working very hard, and we will not quit until this case is solved,” Carlisle said.

<!-- P003 -->
Sometime Tuesday police will begin passing out fliers asking for anyone with information about the murder to come forward and call a special hotline which will be monitored around the clock. That number is 1-800-432-9284. Carlisle expects a reward fund will be set up as well.

<!-- P004 -->
The last person believed to have seen Levi alive, officials say, was the classmate whose home Levi rode his bike to on the afternoon of Oct. 22. He was reportedly on his way home when he disappeared.

<!-- P005 -->
The next morning, Levi’s twin sister, Laci, and their mother, Marilyn Parkman, found the bicycle about a mile from their family’s home. The sixth grader’s body was found in a Dawson County forest around 2:30 p.m. on Thursday.

<!-- P006 -->
Although more than a dozen agents from the Georgia Bureau of Investigation, Forsyth County Sheriff’s Department and Dawson County Sheriff’s Department spent their weekend canvassing the boy’s Burruss Mill Road neighborhood for leads in the case, Carlisle said authorities still haven’t been able to talk to the boy’s father, Milton Frady.

<!-- P007 -->
That’s because they can’t find him. The 37-year-old has been on the run from police since a bench warrant was issued for his arrest in 1995.

<!-- P009 -->
Milton Frady’s conspicuous absence from his son’s funeral Sunday afternoon, and his refusal to help officials in their investigation, continues to fuel speculation about the former Forsyth resident: What, if anything, does he know about his son’s murder?

<!-- P010 -->
Milton Frady’s mother says her son loved Levi and could not have harmed him. She says she hopes police find her grandson’s assailant before her son finds the person responsible himself.

<!-- P011 -->
And although investigators still haven’t talked to Milton Frady, his mother said she has. In a televised interview Sunday night, Milton’s mother said her son wanted to attend young Levi’s funeral but was afraid officials would arrest him on those earlier charges. The grandmother said her Milton felt it wouldn’t help Levi for him to attend the funeral or for him to go to jail. That’s also the reason, she said, that Milton hasn’t contacted the police.

<!-- P012 -->
Dawson County Sheriff Billy Carlisle said Milton Frady can always telephone the sheriff’s department or the tip line if he would like to help them find his son’s killer. “I would encourage him to call us,” Carlisle said.

<!-- P013 -->
The fact that he has been a fugitive from Forsyth officials since he failed to appear in court in 1995 doesn’t lessen the flow of questions. The day he failed to appear in Forsyth County Superior Court he was scheduled to answer charges that he committed the offenses of burglary and contributing to the delinquency of a minor. When he didn’t show up, a bench warrant was issued for his arrest.

<!-- P014 -->
Whether a weapon or other evidence was found at the crime scene, officials are not saying. They continue to remain tight-lipped about the investigation, saying they fear release of specific information might hamper efforts to bring the murderer to justice.
