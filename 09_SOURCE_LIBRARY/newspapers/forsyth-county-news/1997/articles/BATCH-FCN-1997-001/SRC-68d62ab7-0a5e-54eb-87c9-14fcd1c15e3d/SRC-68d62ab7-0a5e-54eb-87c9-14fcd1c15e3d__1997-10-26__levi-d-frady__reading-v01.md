<!-- P001 -->
Levi Dillon Frady, 11, of Cumming, died Thursday, Oct. 23, 1997. Frady was a student at North Forsyth Middle School.

<!-- P002 -->
Survivors include his mother, Marilyn Parkman of Cumming; sister, Laci Frady of Cumming; father, Milton Frady of Cumming; grandparents, Dorsey and Janice Hamby and Lamar and Sarah Frady, all of Cumming; great-grandparents, Burma Gibson and Mandy Allene Hamby, both of Cumming; aunts, uncles and cousins.

<!-- P003 -->
Funeral services will be held Sunday, Oct. 26, at 2 p.m. at Ingram Funeral Home Chapel. The Rev. David Harris will officiate.

<!-- P004 -->
Interment will follow in Sawnee View Garden.

<!-- P005 -->
In lieu of flowers, memorial donations may be made to the Levi Dillon Frady Trust Fund at Citizens Bank, 501 Tri-County Plaza, Cumming, Ga. 30040, or Lanier Bank and Trust, 214 Dahlonega St., Cumming, Ga. 30040.

<!-- P006 -->
Ingram Funeral Home had charge of the arrangements.

<!-- P007 -->
Forsyth County News
October 26, 1997
