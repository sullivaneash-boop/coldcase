## Full extant article text

<!-- P001 -->
The search for a missing North Forsyth Middle School boy came to a sad end on Thursday when the body of 11-year-old Levi Frady was found in Dawson Forest near an abandoned Lockheed plant.

<!-- P002 -->
Friends and neighbors started looking for the boy Thursday morning after his mother, Marilyn Parkman, reported him missing to Forsyth County authorities.

<!-- P003 -->
According to Sheriff’s Office reports, Frady left his Burruss Mill Road home Wednesday afternoon to do homework at the Noah Road home of a friend. He was expected back by 6:30 p.m.

<!-- P004 -->
Parkman went to the Noah Road home around 7:30 p.m. when Frady had not returned.

<!-- P005 -->
The friend’s parents told her Frady had left on his bicycle to eat dinner at another neighbor’s house.

<!-- P006 -->
No one was home at that address when Parkman stopped by.

<!-- P007 -->
She told Sheriff’s Office investigators she fell asleep after going home, but Frady had not returned when she woke up around 2 a.m. She said she thought he spent the night at another friend’s house.

<!-- P008 -->
Friends and neighbors started searching for Frady on Thursday morning.

<!-- P009 -->
Frady’s twin sister, Laci, found her brother’s bicycle in a ditch off Little Mill Road, about a half mile from his house. There was no sign of her brother.

<!-- P010 -->
Investigators from the Sheriff’s Office, the Georgia State Patrol, the Georgia Emergency Management Agency and Forsyth County EMS and firefighters were called in to help look for the boy Thursday, but were forced to stop looking at dark.

<!-- P011 -->
The same day, three hunters from Gilmer County found the body of a young boy in Dawson Forest. On Friday, the body was identified as Frady.

<!-- P012 -->
According to Dawson County Sheriff Billy Carlisle, the boy had been shot.

<!-- P013 -->
Investigators are questioning family and friends about Frady’s disappearance.

<!-- P014 -->

<!-- P015 -->
Carlisle said no one has been arrested in the case, but he said he wants to reassure residents it is not likely there is a random killer of children on the loose.

<!-- P016 -->
“I don’t believe there’s any reason to fear that there’s anyone out abducting young boys,” said Carlisle.

<!-- P017 -->
But that didn’t calm the fears of Forsyth County parents Friday. Sheriff’s Office deputies reported a dramatic increase in the number of parents picking up their children from school Friday afternoon. North Forsyth Middle School offered counseling to students and faculty to help deal with the situation.

<!-- P018 -->
Cumming New Life church has set up a contribution fund for the family. For information on the funds, call Lanier Bank and Trust at 887-1732, Citizens Bank at 886-9500 or Cumming New Life Church at 889-6919.

<!-- P019 -->
Frady’s funeral was planned for Sunday at Ingram Funeral Home Chapel. Interment was to be at Sawnee View Garden.
