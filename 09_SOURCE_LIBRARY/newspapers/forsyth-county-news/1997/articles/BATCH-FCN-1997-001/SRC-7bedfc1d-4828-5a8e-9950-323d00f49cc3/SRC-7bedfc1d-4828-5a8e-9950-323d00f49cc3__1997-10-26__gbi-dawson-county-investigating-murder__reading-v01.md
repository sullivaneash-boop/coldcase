## Full extant article text

<!-- P001 -->
The death of a young Forsyth County sixth grader was no accident. Officials now confirm someone shot the 11-year-old boy and left him to die in a Dawson County forest.

<!-- P002 -->
Although Levi Frady was presumably abducted in Forsyth County — probably near the spot where his bicycle was found, authorities say he was murdered a few miles across the line in Dawson County.

<!-- P003 -->
“It all happened here,” said Dawson County Sheriff Billy Carlisle Friday afternoon, eliminating speculation that the wooded area off Ga. Hwy. 9 might only have been the murderer’s dumping ground.

<!-- P004 -->
When the boy’s assailant is found, Carlisle said, “he or she will be prosecuted here.”

<!-- P005 -->
A state crime lab autopsy has confirmed that the young boy died as a result of one or more gunshot wounds, but Carlisle said details of autopsy findings — such as the caliber of the weapon used and the location of the wounds — are not yet available.

<!-- P006 -->
He would not confirm whether a possible murder weapon has been found or whether a suspect has been identified, but the sheriff did say no warrants have been issued.

<!-- P007 -->
Although Carlisle said agents with the Georgia Bureau of Investigation and Forsyth and Dawson County Sheriff’s Departments have interviewed numerous potential witnesses in the case, he said authorities so far have not felt it necessary to advise anyone

of the constitutionally protected right to remain silent during questioning.

<!-- P010 -->
However, authorities have not yet been able to talk to everyone close to the boy. Carlisle said authorities are actively searching for Levi’s 34-year-old father, Milton Frady. Carlisle stressed Milton is not considered a suspect in the case.

<!-- P011 -->
Instead, Carlisle said the Cumming resident, who is estranged from his son’s mother, is only wanted in connection with previous charges. Carlisle said investigators would like to speak to Milton Frady about his son’s death when he is found.

<!-- P012 -->
Before Levi disappeared, Forsyth officials say they held warrants for Milton Frady’s arrest on non-violent charges, including an alleged burglary, a superior court bench warrant and contributing to the delinquency of a minor.

<!-- P013 -->
He is described as a 5-feet, 11-inch tall white male, weighing approximately 170 pounds. He has shoulder-length brown hair and hazel eyes.

<!-- P014 -->
Parents should not be unduly worried that there is a random child abductor on the loose, Carlisle said: “I don’t think there is a reason to fear that. When something like this happens, statistically speaking, the victim usually knows the perpetrator,” Carlisle said, although he stressed that he is “not saying that is what happened in this case.”

<!-- P015 -->
Very little is being divulged about the crime scene itself, and authorities won’t say whether evidence of sexual abuse was discovered. Carlisle also would not say whether the boy was found wearing each item of clothing he was reportedly dressed in when he was last seen: “I will only say that he was clothed.”

<!-- P016 -->
Whether other potential evidence was found at the scene, he would not confirm nor deny.

<!-- P017 -->
“We don’t want to give out too much information about the crime scene itself to prevent tarnishing the investigation.”

<!-- P018 -->
Dawson County officials were called to the scene around 2:30 p.m. Thursday, after three Gilmer County hunters found the body during the opening day of deer season.

<!-- P019 -->
Carlisle said members of the hunting party left the scene, near the old Lockheed facility on state wildlife management land and went to a nearby home to make the call to authorities.

<!-- P020 -->
When two officers confirmed the report and secured the crime scene, Carlisle said his agents immediately notified the GBI office in Gainesville and requested a crime scene technician be dispatched to the scene.

<!-- P021 -->
When Dawson officials realized the boy’s description matched that of the boy reported missing in an earlier radio-broadcasted look-out, they notified Forsyth officials.

<!-- P022 -->
Carlisle said his department will continue to work with other state and local agencies to find the person or persons responsible for the homicide.
