## Full extant article text

<!-- P001 -->
Why? It’s a difficult question with few consoling answers. Why does a child have to die? And, why so tragic and brutal a death?

<!-- P002 -->
No response can ease the pain of the loss felt by the family, by friends, by schoolmates and teachers of Levi Frady, who is to be laid to rest Sunday. No justification can be found for the senseless murder of a child less than a dozen years old. There should have been so much living ahead for the sixth grader.

<!-- P003 -->
And the ‘what if’ questions also loom. What if he hadn’t had ridden away on his bike, which was found in a ditch near his home? What if it hadn’t been a school night and what if he hadn’t had homework to do which apparently took him to a friend’s house?

<!-- P004 -->
Authorities know a gunshot blast killed the child, but details which have been released are sketchy. Who could have perpetrated such a crime against the bright-eyed youngster? Who would commit such a heinous act — killing and leaving the 11-year-old in the woods?

<!-- P005 -->
Whoever is found to be connected to the murder, there is one thing that no one will ever be able to answer to the satisfaction of the family — and the community. Why?
