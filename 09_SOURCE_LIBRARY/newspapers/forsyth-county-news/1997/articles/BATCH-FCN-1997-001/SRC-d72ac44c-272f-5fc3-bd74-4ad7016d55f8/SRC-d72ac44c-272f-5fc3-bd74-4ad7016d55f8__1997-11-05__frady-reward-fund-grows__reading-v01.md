## Full extant article text

<!-- P001 -->
Reward funds which have been set up for information leading to the arrest of the killer of 11-year-old Levi Frady are growing.

<!-- P002 -->
During its called meeting Monday, the Forsyth County Board of Commissioners voted unanimously to give another $2000 to the reward fund.

<!-- P003 -->
Chairman Bill Jenkins said the board is supporting law enforcement any way they can, and he hopes a reward will help solve the crime.

<!-- P004 -->
Gov. Zell Miller allocated $2,000 to the reward fund. Also, Dawson County Sheriff Billy Carlisle said about $3,500 in donations has been received through his office. Forsyth County Sheriff Denny Hendrix said his department has another $7,000 in reward money for what he describes as the department’s top-priority case. Money has been collected from private businesses and concerned citizens.

<!-- P005 -->
The father of the Forsyth County Middle School sixth grader could be home Wednesday for the first time in nearly three weeks.

<!-- P006 -->
Forsyth County Clerk of Courts Doug Sorrells said the courts are waiting for a motion from Milton Frady’s attorney, Michael Hauptman, for a possible bond hearing Wednesday morning.

<!-- P007 -->
Frady turned himself in to the Forsyth County Sheriff’s Office Oct. 29 and has been in custody since.

<!-- P008 -->
Frady is being held on bench warrants stemming from a 1994 burglary charge.

<!-- P009 -->
But investigators from the Forsyth and Dawson County sheriff’s departments and the Georgia Bureau of Investigation wanted to question Frady about the Oct. 22 murder of his son.

<!-- P010 -->
[Continued on page 2A under REWARD from 1A]

<!-- P011 -->
Levi Frady was found Oct. 23 in a Dawson County forest where he died from a gunshot wound. Investigators have been looking for his killer.

<!-- P012 -->
Milton Frady has been on the run from investigators since the 1994 burglary.

<!-- P013 -->
After Levi’s death, the search for his father intensified. Milton’s whereabouts the night of the murder were unknown.

<!-- P014 -->
Investigators wanted to know what, if anything, he knew about the case.

<!-- P015 -->
While in custody, Frady was questioned by investigators from all three agencies. Dawson County Sheriff Billy Carlisle would not give details about his interview with Frady, but said Frady “cleared up some areas where we had questions.”

<!-- P016 -->
None of the representatives from the three investigative agencies will say Frady has been cleared as a suspect. However, all three maintain everyone in and near the family has been questioned and are considered suspects at this time.

<!-- P017 -->
The investigation was expanded last week to include anyone traveling through the North Forsyth area near Frady’s home and at Dawson Forest Road and Hwy. 9, near the site where three Gilmer County hunters found the child’s body.

<!-- P018 -->
Other hunters on the state wildlife management property that day have been questioned, but the GBI has ruled out an accidental shooting.

<!-- P019 -->
Anyone with information should contact the Dawson County tipline at (800) 432-9284, the Forsyth County Crime Stoppers at 888-7308 or the GBI at 535-5423.
