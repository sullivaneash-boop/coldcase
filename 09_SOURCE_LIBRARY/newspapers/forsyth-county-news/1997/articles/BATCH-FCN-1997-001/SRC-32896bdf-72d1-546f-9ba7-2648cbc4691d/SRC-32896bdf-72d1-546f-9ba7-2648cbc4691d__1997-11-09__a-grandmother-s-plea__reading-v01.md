## Full extant article text

<!-- P001 -->
TO THE EDITOR:

<!-- P002 -->
From the Levi Frady family: We know somebody out there knows something about Levi’s disappearance. It’s so hard to say murder. That’s what it was, murder, and we have to live with that every day of our lives. He was only riding his bike home on Little Mill Road between 6-6:30 p.m. Oct. 22. He was so precious and special but we know that we will never get him back. The only thing we can do for him now is to make sure nobody else has to suffer what Levi went through, at least by this person or persons. It could have been anyone’s child and we don’t want anyone to go through what we’re going through. We didn’t know a person could hurt so bad and still be alive. With God’s help, we will go on but we will never stop hunting his murderer. So please, please, somebody help us. If the reward won’t make your come forth, maybe our cries will.

<!-- P003 -->
The heartbroken family of Levi,
Janice Hamby, Levi’s grandmother

<!-- P004 -->
(If you have information about the Frady case, you are asked to call the hotline established at 800-432-9284; the Georgia Bureau of Investigation at 535-5423 or the Forsyth County Crimestopper line at 888-7308.)
