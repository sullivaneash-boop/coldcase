## Full extant article text

<!-- P001 -->
Milton Frady’s mother Sarah says her son had nothing to do with the murder of his son, sixth grader Levi Frady. During an interview just hours after she brought her son to the Forsyth County Detention Center, Sarah said Milton could not have played a part in her grandson’s death.

<!-- P002 -->
“I don’t see how they could suspect him because he was at home,” she said of the night her grandson disappeared. “When he came home from work, Wednesday, Milton was sitting on the porch.” That would have been about 5:15 p.m. she recalled. “He stayed there — he even called” his children’s home that night, Sarah said. “He calls them every night and every morning.”

<!-- P003 -->
When he called that night, Sarah said he was told his son had gone to a neighbor’s house. Sarah said he called again the next morning and spoke to Laci but said no one told him Levi had not come home until later that day.

<!-- P004 -->
Explaining that Milton loved his children, Sarah said, “He’s been wanting” to come forward since his son was found dead. “He wants the killer found, too.” Sarah said her family wants to help: “That’s why we brought him here,” she said, adding that her son was at the search site Thursday looking for his child with other volunteers.
