FORSYTH COUNTY, Ga. — Nearly 30 years ago on this day, the boy [who later inspired](https://gbi.georgia.gov/services/georgias-public-alert-system/levis-call) "Levi's Call" as an emergency alert in Georgia went missing, and the Georgia Bureau of Investigation is still seeking leads in the case.

Levi Frady, then 11 years old, had gone out on October 22, 1997 to play with friends and was returning home in Forsyth County as it began to get dark. According to the GBI, his house was less than a mile away and he had ridden home on his bike dozens of times.

"Tragically, on this evening, Levi would not make it home. He was abducted, driven to a neighboring county, and brutally murdered. The next day, his body was found in a wooded area," the GBI narrative states. "Law enforcement officials are still searching for his killer."

On Thursday, the [GBI said](https://x.com/GBI_GA/status/2113251981640806843?s=20) they're still working with the sheriff's offices in Dawson and Forsyth counties on the case, and they said the public can still submit tips:

*"Anyone with information about Levi Frady’s death is asked to contact the GBI Tip Line at 1-800-597-TIPS(8477). Anonymous tips can also be submitted online at [https\://gbi.georgia.gov/submit-tips-online](https://t.co/MgVsuYzUKR) or by downloading the See Something, Send Something mobile app."*

The Levi's Call alert in Georgia was established in 2002 as the state-level equivalent of an Amber Alert.

Frady [was last seen on October 22, 1997](https://gbi.georgia.gov/cases/unsolved-homicide/levi-frady) on Little Mill Road in Forsyth County, described by the GBI as a "well-traveled road between Highway 306 and 369."

Two possible witnesses have been sought over the years.

One is described as a man who was walking along Little Mill Road around 6:30 p.m. that day 29 years ago. He was believed to be a white male in his 50s  at the time who walked in a "stooped fashion" with a scruffy, gray beard and wearing a faded blue baseball cap.

The second has been described as a potential witness in the Dawson Forest Wildlife area where Frady's body was found. He was believed to be a white male, 45 to 55 years old then, who was clean shaven with white or gray hair, standing 6 feet or 6-foot-1 with a slim build. He was driving a 1980 to 1985 medium blue Toyota, 2 wheel drive, pick-up truck with a white camper shell.
