Eleven-year-old Levi Frady was found dead in 1997, 20 miles from his home. No one has been found responsible for shooting the child three times. Levi’s death prompted a statewide alert system for child abductions. Levi’s Call is Georgia’s version of the Amber Alert.

By Alexis Stevens

Oct 23, 2019

(_Editor's note: This story posted on ajc.com on Oct. 22, 2017 — the 20th anniversary of Levi Frady's disappearance. The 11-year-old was found dead on Oct. 23, 1997. Although Levi's death prompted Georgia's statewide "Levi's Call" child abduction alert, his killer has never been found. The GBI continues to actively investigate the boy's death, according to the special agent in charge of the case._)

Someone knows what happened to her son. But nearly 20 years after her 11-year-old son was killed, Marilyn Weaver continues to wait for answers.

“There’s still nothing after all this time,” Weaver told The Atlanta Journal-Constitution. “I’ve heard so many rumors. I know that somebody knows. It has to have been more than one person that knows. To me, it’s just mind-boggling.”
