# Research rules

1. Never convert allegations into facts.
2. Never treat AI output as evidence.
3. Every consequential factual claim should eventually have a source. Mark unsourced claims explicitly and queue them for review.
4. Preserve original documents unchanged. Create separate derivatives for OCR, redaction, transcription and annotation.
5. Analysis belongs separately from source evidence.
6. Reddit, Websleuths, Facebook and community rumors are lead generators, not evidence.
7. Explicitly preserve contradictions rather than silently resolving them.
8. Distinguish firsthand sources from hearsay; record the basis of knowledge for each account.
9. Record publication date AND date of the underlying event, including uncertainty in each.
10. Never infer guilt from association.
11. Maintain neutral language concerning living or uncharged individuals.
12. Record uncertainty rather than forcing conclusions.

Keep quotations faithful and locatable. Distinguish verbatim text, paraphrase, translation and interpretation. Source ratings describe source characteristics, not a verdict on a claim. Corroboration requires independent origins; repeated reporting of the same account is not independent confirmation.

Maintain consent, privacy, copyright and access restrictions. Folder names and CSV labels are not access controls. Do not publish private information or upload restricted material to AI services without an appropriate handling decision. This archive is a research catalog, not an official evidence repository or proof of physical custody.

Record substantive changes, authorship and reasons in `change_log/`. Preserve previous statement versions and prior assessments before revising them. No automated process should promote an AI suggestion into an accepted claim or resolve a contradiction.
