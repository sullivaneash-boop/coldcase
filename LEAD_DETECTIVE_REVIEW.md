## Current reconstruction

Levi was alone at home after 4:12 p.m. on Wed 22 Oct 1997 and spent the afternoon cycling between two friends' homes. He left his book bag on friend #2's patio. At 6:38 he phoned home: it was getting dark and he was afraid of the dogs. He was told to come straight home. **Last firm sighting: 6:50 p.m. on Little Mill Road**, heading toward friend #2's (empty) home; sunset was about 6:55. He never reached home, which was less than a mile away.

**The bicycle is an object eventually found, not a clock.** It is firmly placed only from 5:15 a.m., at a driveway edge. Laci's "bike that looks like" Levi's, seen during a search that ended home by 7:34, is POSSIBLE only: it is a resemblance, it is not tied to the same driveway, and it is absent from the earliest rendition of the 2004 chronology. The motorist's and the mother's non-sightings are weak negatives. **Separation most probably happened that evening** (a short ride never completed), **but no upper bound before 5:15 a.m. is established.**

His mother was told at about 7:30 that he had gone to eat with another family. She believed it, searched at 2 a.m. and reported him at 8 a.m. Before the report, children moved the bicycle into the ditch, and relatives took it home and decided to return it. The return and the police arrival are not timestamped. He was found shot at about 2 p.m. on 23 Oct in Dawson Forest; the Dawson sheriff said he was killed there. **The time of death is unknown; no public medical basis narrows the 6 p.m.–2:30 p.m. interval.**

## Most important facts

1. Date, place and finders of the body; gunshot death; killing site stated by the sheriff as Dawson Forest (CONFIRMED / STRONGLY SUPPORTED).
2. Last firm sighting 6:50 p.m.; bicycle at a driveway edge by 5:15 a.m. (PROBABLE).
3. The bicycle was handled at least twice before police, and possibly a third time on its return (PROBABLE).
4. "A couple of shell casings" were reported by Forsyth Sheriff Paxton (2002). **Recovery location and any link to the shooting are UNKNOWN**; a 2007 article places some in the Little Mill ditch.
5. Two possible witnesses are still publicly sought: the 6:30 walker and the Toyota/camper man seen at about 10 a.m. on the 23rd. Seeking a witness says nothing about the time of death.

## Critical uncertainties

- When Levi was separated from the bicycle, and when he died.
- Where the casings were found, and whether any shot was fired at the bicycle site.
- Whether any physical evidence exists **today** and is suitable for testing. Nothing is confirmed after 2004.
- Laci's earliest account: exact driveway, and whether she told anyone that night.
- Whether the driveway man (6:52–7:00) was identified. Not publicly identified ≠ never identified.

## Things that don't fit

- Levi was heading toward an empty house at dusk after being told to come straight home.
- The relatives decided to return the bicycle to the ditch, which is self-defeating as concealment.
- "A couple" of casings against three shots, with no stated location.
- The man at the driveway is not among the publicly sought witnesses.
- The earliest dated rendition of the chronology omits Laci's sighting.
