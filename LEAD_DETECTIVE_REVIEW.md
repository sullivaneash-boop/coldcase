## Current reconstruction

Levi was alone at home after 4:12 p.m. on Wed 22 Oct 1997 and spent the afternoon cycling between two friends' homes. He left his book bag on friend #2's patio. At 6:38 he phoned home: it was getting dark and he was afraid of the dogs. He took the Little Mill Road route and was seen there at 6:50, heading toward friend #2's (empty) home. Sunset was about 6:55 p.m. EDT. A lost motorist saw no bicycle at 6:52–7:00 but did see a man walk into the woods at a driveway a few hundred feet from the eventual bicycle spot. Laci saw a bicycle like his at a driveway at 7:00–7:15. **Best estimate of when he was taken: about 6:50–7:15 p.m.** His mother was told at 7:30 that he had gone to eat with another family. She believed it, searched at 2 a.m. and reported him at 8 a.m. Before the report, the bicycle was moved by a resident's children and then by relatives, who took it home and put it back. He was found shot at about 2 p.m. on 23 Oct, the opening day of rifle season, in Dawson Forest near the old Lockheed site, 15+ road miles away. The sheriff said he was killed there. **Whether he died that evening or the next morning is unknown.**

## Most important facts

1. Date, place and finders of the body; gunshot death; killed at the Dawson scene (CONFIRMED / STRONGLY SUPPORTED, 1997 official).
2. Last sighting at 6:50 on Little Mill Road; bicycle down by 5:15 a.m. at the latest, possibly by 7:15 p.m. (PROBABLE, GBI chronology).
3. The bicycle was handled at least three times before police took it (PROBABLE).
4. Physical evidence is thin but preserved: "a couple" of casings, the bicycle (GBI custody in 2004), autopsy material, and clothing (PROBABLE).
5. Two possible witnesses are still sought: a 6:30 walker on Little Mill Road, and a Toyota/camper man in the forest at about 10 a.m. on the 23rd (CONFIRMED that they are sought).

## Critical uncertainties

- Time of death: evening of the 22nd or morning of the 23rd.
- Wound pattern and weapon class (three shots is probable; the rest is rumor).
- Whether a sexual assault occurred (officially withheld; secondary accounts conflict).
- Whether the driveway man (6:52–7:00) was ever identified.
- Where the book bag was, and why Levi was heading toward friend #2's home.

## Possible bias / tunnel vision

Early "the victim usually knows the killer" framing pointed the investigation at the family circle. Interagency friction (scene access, unshared photos and polygraphs). A public window ("6–7 p.m.") that is earlier than the investigators' own chronology. Media and blog drift (distances, casings in the ditch, "execution-style", a negation inserted into a quote). PROJECT LEVI: flat MEDIUM confidence and effort spent on peripheral bookkeeping. Our own risk: over-trusting the detailed 2004 chronology.

## Things that don't fit

- The investigators kept seeking a man seen 15 hours after the abduction.
- Levi was heading toward an empty house at dusk after being told to come straight home.
- The relatives returned the bicycle to the ditch (self-defeating as concealment; fits panic or wanting police to see it in place).
- "A couple" of casings against three shots.
- The man at the driveway is not among the published witnesses.
